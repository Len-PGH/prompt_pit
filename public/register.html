What you need to compete

Bring your own / set up in advance:

  • Your AI coding assistant of choice (Claude Code, etc.) — logged in and ready.
  • Your laptop + charger, and a GitHub account.
  • A SignalWire Space — this is not provided. Sign up free and have a phone number + API credentials ready before you arrive.
  • A backup plan. Prompts fail. Recover with style.
